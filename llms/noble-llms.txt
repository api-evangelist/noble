# Noble

> Noble (operated by Benga Inc.) is a no-code risk and credit decisioning platform providing risk infrastructure for the full customer lifecycle. Fintechs, lenders, and banks use Noble to verify identity, aggregate and assess credit and financial-health data, build automated underwriting/decisioning workflows with a no-code Workflow Builder, and monitor creditworthiness over time. Noble is delivered as a SaaS platform, an API, and an embeddable widget. Founded 2021, San Mateo, CA; a Y Combinator company backed by Insight Partners, Paradigm, and Polychain.
